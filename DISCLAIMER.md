images and animations. Those are usually protected by copyright, trademark or
license terms that belong to the site owner.

**Intended use:** learning how sites are built, prototyping, design references,
migrating or rebuilding sites you own or are authorized to work on, and
accessibility or performance audits.

off their brand, phishing or impersonation, or circumventing paywalls, logins

site's terms of service before cloning.
